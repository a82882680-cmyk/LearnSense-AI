    How the score is built

    Rule-based and transparent. The weights were chosen by our group and are experimental.

    SignalWeightRule
    Completion time{{ weights.time }}(actual ÷ expected) − 1, limited to 0–1. Double the expected time or more gives the full weight.
    Unsuccessful attempts{{ weights.unsuccessful }}unsuccessful ÷ attempts. All attempts unsuccessful gives the full weight.
    Hints requested{{ weights.hints }}hints ÷ 5, limited to 1.
    Repeated attempts{{ weights.attempts }}(attempts − 1) ÷ 5, limited to 0–1.

    Index = sum of (weight × fraction), rounded, always kept between 0 and 100.

    Suggestions use simple thresholds: time at 1.5× expected or more, at least 2 unsuccessful attempts making up half or more of the attempts, 3 or more hints, and a teacher check-in when two or more signals appear together or the index is 75 or above.
