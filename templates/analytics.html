Activity is not the same as understanding

We separate activity indicators (what a learner does) from performance indicators (what a learner shows in an assessment).

SIMULATED SAMPLE DATA — NOT REAL STUDENT RECORDS

Activity indicators

  • Attendance – how many sessions were attended.
  • Assignment completion – how much assigned work was submitted.
  • Revision activity – how many revision sessions were completed (out of 10).

They show participation. They do not prove the content was understood.

Performance indicator

  • Quiz score – marks out of 10 in a quiz.

It shows how a learner did on one quiz. One quiz is a small snapshot, so it should be read with care.

How the combined score is calculated
