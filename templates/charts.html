What the sample data looks like

Six interactive charts drawn with Chart.js from the dataset. Hover over a bar or point to see its value.

SIMULATED SAMPLE DATA — NOT REAL STUDENT RECORDS

1. Attendance vs Assignment Completion

S01 combines high attendance (92%) with high assignment completion (85%). S05 completed 80% of assignments with 70% attendance, while S04 attended 88% of sessions but completed only 55% of assignments. Attendance alone does not tell us how much work was completed.

2. Quiz Performance Comparison

Quiz scores range from 2/10 to 8/10. Bars in coral are below 5/10, which marks where extra support might be considered. A single quiz is only a snapshot.

3. Revision Activity vs Quiz Scores

S04 completed 9 of 10 revision sessions yet scored 3/10, while S05 completed 6 and scored 7/10. With five records we only describe this pattern. We cannot say revision causes or fails to cause higher scores.

4. Combined Learning-Progress Score

The highlighted bar is the highest score. The scores use our experimental weights (25/30/20/25) and are an illustration, not a validated measure.

5. Indicator-wise Student Comparison

Each line is one student across the four indicators (quiz and revision as percentages). Lines that dip sharply at Quiz show a gap between activity and performance.

6. Performance Distribution

How many students fall in each combined-score band. With five students this is only a small illustration of a distribution.
